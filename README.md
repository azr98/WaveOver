CURRENT PROJECT - This web app gives 2 people a 3 day timer to write down what they want to say to each other after an argument.Each is sent what the other wrote, by email, after the 3 day timer ends. Not after and not before. This webapp is built with next js and deployed vercel and uses aws


The Web app works like this : Person 1 submits the email of person 2 and a discussion topic. Person 2 is invited to signup and ‘accept’ the discussion. 

Both are then given a text editor in the web app and a 3 day displayed timer to write what they want to say. After the 3 day timer is up each what each person wrote is sent to the other by email at the same time automatically. Both people receive automatic email reminders two days one day , 12 hours and four hours before the 3 day deadline. 

The Web app solves the pain of deep or difficult conversation and topic that person 1 wants to talk about thoroughly with person 2 without it quickly spiralling into a heated argument which happens in face to face conversation. 

The web app makes the communication written and since the responses are sent at the same time it prevents emotional chain comeback based arguments. The three day timer forces both of them to keep rethinking and re-editing so that unwanted emotions die down and only what they accurately convey is written and sent to the other.  

This app would mainly be used by romantic couples but could definitely be used by friends, family members or even work colleagues to ideate or debate a solution.
